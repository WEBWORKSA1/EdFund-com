Privacy policy

Last updated {{today}}

What we collect

Information you submit in forms (such as your name, email, education level, country and optional phone number), plus basic usage data collected by cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To send the matches, plans, alerts and replies you ask for
  • To run giveaways and contact winners
  • To improve the site and measure performance
  • With your explicit opt-in only, to share your details with vetted education and lending partners so they can send you relevant offers

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We never ask for Social Security numbers, bank details or card numbers in our forms.

Service providers

Forms are delivered through a secure form-processing service. Donations are processed by third-party payment providers (such as Stripe or PayPal). Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.

Cookies & advertising

We may use Google AdSense and Google Analytics. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. The cookie banner lets you limit non-essential cookies.

Children

EdFund is intended for users aged 13 and older. Giveaway entrants must be 16 or older, or have a parent's permission.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or unsubscribe, at any time through our contact form. Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA), you may have additional rights.
