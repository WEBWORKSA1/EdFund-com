Education should be funded by opportunity, not by debt

EdFund puts scholarships, grants, smart borrowing and saving in one clear, free place, so no student skips school, or overpays for it, because the information was too hard to find.

🎯

Mission

Help every student find the lowest-cost path to their education: free money first, then smart loans.

🔍

Editorial standards

We link every listing to its official source, cite government data, date our rates and fix errors quickly. Report an error.

💰

How we make money

Ads, disclosed partner referrals, sponsors and donations. Partners never pay for favorable reviews. Disclosure.

What we'll never do

  • Charge students to search or apply
  • Sell your personal information
  • Hide paid placements
  • Ask for payment to "unlock" a scholarship. That's a scam red flag.

What we're building next

  • State-by-state and major-by-major scholarship pages
  • Personal accounts with deadline reminders
  • A school directory with net-price data
  • Live FAFSA workshops on YouTube
