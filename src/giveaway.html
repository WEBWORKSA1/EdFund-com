Official rules: EdFund $1,000 No-Essay Scholarship

Last updated {{today}}

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

1. Sponsor

The operator of EdFund.com ("Sponsor"). Contact the Sponsor through the contact form.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least 16 years old at the time of entry and who are currently enrolled, or plan to enroll within 12 months, in an accredited high school, college, university, graduate or vocational program. Entrants under the age of majority need permission from a parent or guardian. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate families are not eligible. The drawing is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Entry period

Each monthly drawing runs from 12:00:00 a.m. ET on the first day of the calendar month to 11:59:59 p.m. ET on the last day of that month.

4. How to enter

Complete the online entry form on this page. Limit one (1) base entry per person per monthly period. You can earn bonus entries as described on this page. Referral bonus entries require the referred person to submit a valid, eligible entry. Entries created with automated, duplicate or fraudulent methods are void.

5. Prize

One (1) prize of US $1,000 per monthly drawing, paid to the winner's school for education costs or, at the Sponsor's discretion, directly to the winner. Approximate retail value: US $1,000. The winner is responsible for any applicable taxes.

6. Winner selection & odds

A winner is selected by random drawing from all eligible entries within 15 days after each entry period ends. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

7. Notification

The potential winner is notified by email and must respond within 14 days and provide proof of eligibility and enrollment. If not, an alternate winner may be selected.

8. Privacy

Entry information is used as described in our privacy policy. Partner communications are sent only to entrants who opt in.

9. General

By entering, participants agree to these rules and to the Sponsor's decisions. The Sponsor may modify or cancel the promotion if fraud or technical failure affects its integrity. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, any social media platform.
