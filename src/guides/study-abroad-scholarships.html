Your 12-month timeline

  1. 12 months out: shortlist 5–8 programs; check eligibility (work experience, return-home requirements).
  2. 9 months: take language tests (IELTS/TOEFL) and contact potential supervisors or programs.
  3. 6 months: draft essays and ask for references.
  4. 3 months: finalize and submit; prepare for interviews.

How to stand out

  • Leadership with evidence: what you led, what changed, by how much.
  • A clear "why this country, why this course": specific modules, professors and outcomes.
  • Your return plan: many programs want to see impact at home.
  • Practice the interview: mock panels with feedback.

Documents checklist

  • Passport
  • Transcripts & degree certificates
  • Language test scores
  • CV / résumé
  • 2–3 references
  • Personal statement / essays
  • University admission (for some programs)
