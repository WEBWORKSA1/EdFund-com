Advertiser disclosure

Last updated {{today}}

EdFund is free for students because it is supported by:

  • Display advertising (e.g. Google AdSense), labeled "Advertisement."
  • Partner referrals. We may receive compensation when you request offers from, or are matched with, lenders, schools or education services. This can affect where and how partner offers appear.
  • Sponsorships. Named scholarships, contest prizes and sponsored content are clearly labeled.
  • Donations from individuals and organizations.

What compensation doesn't affect: official data (such as federal loan rates), our editorial guides, or which third-party scholarships we list. Scholarship listings link directly to the official provider.

EdFund is not a lender and doesn't make credit decisions. Rates and terms offered by lenders depend on your credit profile and the lender's criteria.
