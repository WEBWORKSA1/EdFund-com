FAQ

Questions students ask us

Is EdFund really free?
Yes. Students and families never pay. We're supported by advertising, partner referrals (clearly disclosed), sponsors and donations. See our advertiser disclosure.
Is EdFund legit?
Every listing links to the official provider so you apply directly at the source. We never ask for payment to apply, and we never sell your personal information.
Will checking loan options affect my credit?
No. Asking EdFund for loan options does not run a credit check. Lenders use a soft inquiry to show prequalified rates. A hard inquiry only happens if you choose to formally apply with a lender.
How does the $1,000 giveaway work?
Enter once per month in about 30 seconds. One winner is drawn at random after the month ends and notified by email. No purchase or essay is needed. See the official rules.
Can international students use EdFund?
Yes. Our directory includes fully funded international programs in the UK, Europe, Asia and Australia, plus guidance on loans that don't need a U.S. cosigner.
