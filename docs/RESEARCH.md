## What we took from them (built into EdFund.com)

1. Matching quiz and multi-step forms: the easy questions come first, contact details come last, and a progress bar shows how far along you are.
2. A scholarship database you can filter by country, level, category, no-essay and amount, plus a saved list.
3. A monthly no-essay drawing with a public rules page, bonus entries for referrals, and a winners wall.
4. A loan section with the official, dated federal rates, a soft-pull rate check, and a disclosure.
5. Calculators whose results link straight to the matching lead form.
6. Donations: preset amounts, a monthly toggle, an impact line for each amount, and a breakdown of where the money goes.
7. A sponsor-a-scholarship offer and advertising packages.
8. Trust signals: sources, a methodology page, the "is it legit" question answered, a disclosure, and a privacy page.
9. Video and guides built for search traffic and YouTube.
