Student loan FAQ

How much should I borrow?
A common rule: keep total student debt at or below your expected first-year salary. Use our affordability calculator.
Do I need a cosigner?
Most federal student loans don't need one. For private loans, students with limited credit usually need a creditworthy cosigner to qualify or get a lower rate.
Can international students get loans?
Some private lenders serve international students, often requiring a U.S. cosigner. A few lenders don't require one for certain schools and programs. Tell us about your situation in the form above.
