Donation FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?
EdFund is currently not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. If that changes, we'll update this page and issue receipts.
Can I cancel a monthly donation?
Yes, anytime. Use the link in your payment receipt, or contact us and we'll cancel it for you.
How do I know the money is used well?
We publish quarterly impact updates with prize payouts, tools shipped and students reached.
